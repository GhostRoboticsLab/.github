## 🧭 What we do

**GhostLabs** is an independent research lab and engineering studio working at the seam between **physical systems and learning machines** — locomotion, control, and the models that make them adaptive. We build robots, the intelligence that drives them, and the applied systems in between.

We do our own research, and we take on **custom builds** for partners who need that same rigor pointed at their problem.
