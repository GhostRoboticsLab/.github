## `◇ HOW WE PRACTISE`

**Four commitments we make to every engagement.**

- **`01` We commit to finished work, not deliverables.** An engagement ends when something works in production for the people who commissioned it.
- **`02` Most of what we build stays private.** Client repositories never appear in public. What's surfaced is internal research and the work we're cleared to show.
- **`03` Direct contact with the people writing the code.** No account managers, no relays. The engineers who read your brief are the engineers who ship the result.
- **`04` We say no when we should.** We work on a small number of projects at a time, and we say so on the first call when a brief sits outside our practice.

**`Brief`** → **`Discovery`** → **`Build`** → **`Ship`** — one team, from first letter to production.
